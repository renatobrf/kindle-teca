# Scribbles and Notes

My reading method is steady and disciplined: usually one chapter each day, every day of the week. I always keep a highlighter close at hand to mark the ideas that stand out, and for each book I open a new text document with notes organized according to the same chapter structure used by the author.

Over time, **e-books** emerged as an evolution in this search for content, offering significant savings in the acquisition of new titles and immediate access to their material.
